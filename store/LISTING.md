# Chrome Web Store listing copy

**Name:** Tempo — Tab & Window Refresh

**Summary:** Refresh a tab or just one Chrome window on your schedule. Simple intervals, pause/resume, and right-click controls. No tracking.

**Suggested category:** Workflow & Planning

**Language:** English (United States)

**Distribution visibility:** Public — searchable in the Chrome Web Store.

## Detailed description

Tempo keeps the page you need fresh, with a clear timer and simple controls.

Refresh one tab, or the tabs in just one Chrome window. Choose 30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes, or 15 minutes, or enter a custom interval from 30 seconds to 24 hours. Close the popup and the timer keeps running.

• This tab: the timer stays with your selected tab when you switch tabs.
• This window: refresh the current tabs in only that window, including tabs you open later. Other windows stay untouched.
• Pause, resume, update, or stop at any time.
• Bypass Chrome's cache when you need fresh content.
• Right-click a webpage or the Tempo icon for quick interval and timer controls.
• See a countdown and a toolbar badge showing which timer is active.
• Clean light and dark themes that follow your system preference.

No account. No payments. No ads. No tracking. No remotely loaded code.

Tempo does not read page content or request access to all websites. It stores interval preferences on your device and active timer state only for the current browser session. Selected tab titles and URLs are used temporarily in memory and are not stored or transmitted.

Chrome may delay refreshes when your computer is busy or asleep. Tabs still loading are skipped for that cycle. Timers end when you close Chrome, reload/disable/update the extension, or close the target. Refreshing can discard unsaved edits, so pause before typing or editing. Chrome's native tab-strip menu cannot be customized by this extension; right-click a webpage or the toolbar icon instead.

Requires Chrome 120 or newer. Tempo is an independent utility.
